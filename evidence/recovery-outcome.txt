Recovery outcome
================

The documented L70 01.08 preprocessing model was validated on the original HP ZBook 17 research system.

Outcome:

- the offline-recovered physical-key candidate was accepted by firmware setup;
- the BIOS administrator password was removed successfully;
- the machine then accepted the official HP BIOS update from the previously prepared USB media;
- no firmware binary, real password hash, recovered password, or recovered physical-key sequence is disclosed here.

This confirms that the recovered SHA-1 preimage corresponded to the firmware's actual administrator-password verifier on the tested system, not merely to an offline reimplementation.

The exact updated BIOS revision should be recorded separately after an operating-system-side version check.
